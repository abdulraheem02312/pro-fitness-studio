Privacy

What this website does with your information. Short version: we don't collect it.

What we collect

Nothing is stored by this website. There are no accounts, no tracking and no advertising cookies.

Forms and WhatsApp

The contact form, membership builder, quiz and calculators run only in your browser. When you press a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a message ready. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp yourself. After that, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.

Other services this site loads

Fonts come from Google Fonts, the map on the contact page comes from Google Maps, and animation code loads from public code servers (cdnjs, unpkg and jsDelivr). These services can see your IP address when they send the files, like any website.
